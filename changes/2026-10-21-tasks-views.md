- Feature: A project's Tasks tab can be drawn in five views, chosen from the gear at its top right
  List is the tab as it was, and stays the one it opens in until you choose another.
  Table is a table with a column for each of a task's do-date, deadline, priority, estimate and owner, under a heading for each workstream.
  List and details is a dense list with the task you pick shown in full in a pane beside it, its fields, its description and Mark done.
  Board gives each workstream a column of cards: drag a card to another column to move it into that workstream, and Left and Right go from column to column.
  By do-date sorts the open tasks into Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, Next 7 days, Later and No do-date: drag a task onto one to give it that day, or write one there already dated.
  The view you choose is kept for every project's Tasks tab, on every device.
  Every view works from the keyboard as the List does, edits a task in place, and opens as you left it, its folds and the sub-tasks you showed already in place.
  In every view but the List, Show completed in the toolbar brings in the last 14 days' finished work.
  A task dropped back on its own list, or between lists, now stays where it was.
  A deadline passed on a task still open now shows in a red badge, where before it looked like one only due soon.
  Enter or Space on a task's Today, Tomorrow or day button now presses that button, rather than editing the task or opening its description.
