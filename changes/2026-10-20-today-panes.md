- Major: Today is laid out in panes: the plan and the habits, the notes, the timeline
  The header is one band: the day, its date, and the day's facts as chips beside them (what is on now and next, how you slept, where you stay).
  At the left, the Plan pane holds the nudges, the tasks for today, and those carried over from before, each a row that folds with its group.
  A task's box writes it in the notes, ticked; its calendar moves it to another day, and Reschedule moves everything carried over at once, with Undo.
  Under the plan, the Habits pane lists the habits due today with their box, their last seven days and their streak; a box writes or ticks the habit in the notes.
  The notes take the middle, the timeline the right, each pane scrolling inside itself; Alt+P folds the plan away and Ctrl+\ the timeline, the notes taking their room.
