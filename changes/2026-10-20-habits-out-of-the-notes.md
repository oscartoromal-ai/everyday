- Major: Habits leave the notes: ticked and commented on in their pane, each with a page and a file of its own
  The notes no longer hold habits: "/habit" is gone from them, and the habit rows written before are moved out, their days and comments kept.
  In the Habits pane beside the notes, a box ticks a habit for the day, and its name opens the day's comment under its row (Enter saves it); a comment on a day not ticked says why it was skipped.
  A click on one of its last seven days, or Left and Right on its box, picks that day for the boxes and comments, so a day missed can be filled in.
  New habit, at the pane's foot, starts one; All habits is the new Habits page in the sidebar, every habit over its last four weeks.
  A habit's own page has its days, its half year, and its words: what it is and its log, a comment a day, written there as a document and kept in the everyday files as habits/<name>.md.
  Calendar's sheet ticks habits without writing in the notes, and its habit columns open their pages.
