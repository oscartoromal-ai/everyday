- Feature: Today's panes are yours to arrange: move the plan, the habits, the notes or the timeline to another place or column, hide any, and show it again
  Drag a pane by its title bar: a spark line shows where it will land, in its column or another; a column not showing gets a zone at its edge, and a pane dropped there makes it.
  On hover, a grip at a title bar's left edge and an eye after its name: the eye hides the pane (the toast offers Undo), and Tab to the grip moves the pane with the arrow keys, or hides it with Delete.
  Panes, the new icon at the day's header, ticks each pane shown: a click hides or shows one, back in its place, and Set them back as they were puts every pane where it started.
  A column with no pane left in it goes, the others taking its room; the layout holds on every device and every day's page.
  Alt+P and Ctrl+\ now fold the left and the right column away, whatever is in them, their buttons named for it ("Fold Plan and Habits away").
