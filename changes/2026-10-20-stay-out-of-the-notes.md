- Major: Where you stay leaves the notes for a Stay pane of its own on Today
  The notes no longer hold stays: "/stay" is gone from them, and the stays written before are moved out, each item kept as it was; a day's notes that held only its stay have no file now.
  In the Stay pane, under the habits, write the place and the accommodation in place, and tick accommodation once it is sorted; Enter goes on to the next item.
  A place is offered as you wrote it before, so it is written the same way each time.
  A day with no stay offers the last one again ("Same as yesterday"), and Carry on gives the day's stay to the days after it (the next day, to Sunday, a week on, or a date you pick), with Undo.
  Item, at the pane's foot, adds an item of your own, and its × takes one away.
  Under the date, the stay reads as before, accommodation in amber until sorted; a click goes to it in the pane. Calendar's sheet writes the same stay.
