- Feature: Write a project's AGENTS.md, and its other documents, in Markdown drawn as it reads, as a task's description is
  Headings, lists, boxes, quotes, tables, code and links are drawn as they read, their Markdown showing where the caret is.
- Feature: Switch a document or a task's description between Live, Source and Reading at the page's top right, or Ctrl+E in and out of Reading
  Each kind of page opens again in the mode last chosen on it; Reading still ticks boxes and opens links.
