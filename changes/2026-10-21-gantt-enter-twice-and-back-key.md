- Feature: On a Gantt, Enter twice quickly opens the task on its page
  On a task's Task, Start, length or Due cell, a second Enter straight after the first opens the task, as Ctrl+Enter does, with the cell left as it was.
  A slower second Enter keeps what is written and goes down, as before.
- Feature: Ctrl+Backspace goes back to the page before
  It does what Back and Alt+Left do, from any page; in a field it still deletes a word.
