Input fields: a future Japan, as 90s anime drew it

The same inputs in each: the task composer at rest and while writing, a form row (text, select, date), and its buttons. Day on the left, Night on the right. The tech is in the details: cut corners, corner ticks, a lit baseline. Nothing else on the page changes.

1. MAGI terminal

Evangelion's command terminals, quietened. Fields are chamfered plates, cut corners instead of rounded ones, with one warm light for the field you are in. Night is amber on black; Day is the same plate printed in vermilion on warm paper.

2. Section 9

Ghost in the Shell's displays. A field is barely there, four corner ticks and a baseline, until you are in it: then the ticks and baseline light and a data rule with index marks runs along its top. Night is phosphor on deep teal with scanlines you have to look for; Day is the same HUD on pale mint glass.

Night

3. Settei sheet

The model sheets animators drew mecha from (Patlabor, Macross). Fields are drafted rather than styled: a thin rule, registration crosses at the corners, a leader line to each label, and the field in use re-marked in red pencil. Night is the cyanotype of the same sheet.
