- Feature: Open a task's page from wherever tasks are listed
  A row on a project's Tasks or on Plan has Open beside its bin (Ctrl+Enter on the cursor's row), and the task being edited there has Open among its fields (Ctrl+Shift+Enter), saved first.
  A task in Today's notes has Open after its icons once it is added (Ctrl+Shift+Enter in it adds it first if need be), and a nudge's Open goes to its task's page.
- Feature: A task's page goes back to any page it was opened from, named: Garmin, Spending, a project's document and the rest, not just Today, Calendar, Plan and a project's pages
- Feature: Today's notes are Markdown, drawn as they read by the same engine as a task's description and a project's documents
  Headings, bold and italics, code, links, lists, quotes, rules, code blocks and tables, their marks showing where the caret is; a list goes on with Enter and nests with Tab.
  A task's description rows, a reflection and a habit's note draw their words' Markdown too; a task's title stays the plain words it is everywhere else.
  Live, Source and Reading at the day's header (Ctrl+E in and out of Reading), remembered for the notes.
