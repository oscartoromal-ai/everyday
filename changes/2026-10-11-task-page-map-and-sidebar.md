- Feature: A task's page takes the whole window, with its project's map at the left and its agent work in the sidebar at the right
  The task is now a wide document instead of a narrow centred column.
  At its left, a map of its project: its context's documents, then its workstreams with their open tasks, the task marked where it stands; each opens its page, and folds are remembered.
  At its right, the sidebar holds the task's Agent work, its Outline (the description's headings, each a way to its line) and its History beside the agent window, chosen by a strip of small icons that stays when the sidebar collapses.
  Agent work shows only once the task is Claude's: your own task has Hand to an agent there, and Claude's lists its brief, Launch, pull requests, outcome and updates in the order the work goes.
