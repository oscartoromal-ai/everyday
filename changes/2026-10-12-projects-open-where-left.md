- Feature: A project opens again where you left it
  Left on its Gantt, its Tasks or one of its documents, a project opened again from the sidebar, Projects or Ctrl+K comes back to that page, as it was: scrolled where it was, a Gantt with the same weeks and rows in view and its cursor on the same cell.
  While you are in the project, its tabs and its name in the sidebar open what they always did.
  A document deleted since opens the project on its Overview instead.
