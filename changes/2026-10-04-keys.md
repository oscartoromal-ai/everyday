- Feature: Choose every key: Keyboard, from Settings, and keys.toml
  Every action a key does is listed on Keyboard, with its keys: + takes the next key you press, × takes one away, and Default puts them back.
  The keys you choose are kept in `keys.toml` in the app's home, which you can also write by hand in any text editor; the app takes it up as it changes, and says on Keyboard what in it could not be used.
  The keys dialog (?), the pages' hints and the buttons' titles say the keys as you chose them, and two actions on one key are marked as a clash.
  Anything else can read and set them too, at `/ui/keys`.
- Feature: Keys for what was the mouse's alone
  A new project, document, weigh-in, bill, goal, account or category (Shift+A); sync the work calendar, Garmin, GitHub or the models (R); back to today, this week or this month (T); find a repository (/); join the meeting (Alt+J); delete a task or document on its page (Ctrl+-).
  The theme (Ctrl+Shift+L), the sidebar (Ctrl+Shift+B), Settings (Ctrl+,), the update (Ctrl+Shift+U), and Forward (Alt+Right).
  On a task's page, each field's menu: do date, due, repeat, estimate, priority, project, owner, status and History (Alt+D, U, R, E, I, P, O, S, H).
  Today's carried tasks (Alt+C), a Gantt's filter (F), and a project's overview, tasks and Gantt (Shift+O, Shift+T, Shift+G); Ctrl+Enter on a task list (a project's Tasks, Plan) opens the task's page, as it does on a Gantt.
  Write to Claude on Nutrition and Spending (M), and import a bank statement on Accounts (I).
