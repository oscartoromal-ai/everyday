    Writing a task

    Enter

    Back to the list (an edit saves)
    Tab

    Today

    - [ ] Space
    At a row's start: a task, written in the notes
    /reflection
    At a row's start: a reflection, free text
    /habit name
    At a row's start: the habit, done today (untick: skipped)
    /stay
    At a row's start: where you stay today, its items (place, accommodation) ticked once sorted
    Tab
    From a task's title to the row under it; a row into the task above it, or into a sub-task
    Shift Tab
    A step back out: a sub-task to a row, a row out of its task
    friday Space
    In a task's title, as in the task sheet: words become its fields (a title's last words as it is closed, the toast saying what they became, with Undo); Backspace right after, or Ctrl Z any time, puts the words back

    Close the task (it is added), onto the next row or where it is
    Ctrl Enter
    Close a reflection, onto the next row

    Ctrl \

    Esc
    Out of the notes (in a task, once it is saved: Esc again)
