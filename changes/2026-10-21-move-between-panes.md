- Feature: Ctrl+Alt and an arrow move between panes, wherever there are some
  On Today, from the plan to the notes to the timeline, and up and down a column; on a task's page, between its map, the task and the sidebar's pane; and out to the sidebar at the left or into the agent window at the right, from any page.
  The focus goes back to where it was in the pane: the notes at their caret, a column at the pane you were in last.
  It works while writing too, in the notes or a description, and the pane's edge lights up a moment to show where you went.
  The keys are yours to change on Keyboard, under Panes.
